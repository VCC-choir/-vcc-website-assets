Roast Bake Draw

Every bake starts with coconut. Tap your team, then draw what makes yours unique.
Before you draw:
  • Each team must appoint one person to do the draw. If a team draws under any card other than their own, that team will be penalized.
  • Tap the word "Team" on your card to type in your official team name — do this first, before drawing.
Each team taps their own card to reveal their ingredient.
